ETSY DESIGN TOOL - HUONG DAN NHANH

1. Mo "Etsy Design Tool" ngoai Desktop.
2. Bam "Them thu muc..." chon thu muc mockup (hoac keo tha thu muc vao bieu tuong Desktop).
3. Bam BAT DAU. Xong bam "Mo to xem lai" de soat.

Ket qua: <thu muc mockup>\Design Claude\<ten mockup>.png  (4200x4800, 300 dpi, nen trong suot)
- Mau da co design trong thu muc con "Design" thi tu bo qua.
- Mau da co file trong "Design Claude" thi bo qua; muon lam lai: tich "Lam lai" hoac xoa file do.
- Ao den: tool tu xoa van vai. In len ao mau sang: tich "Giu phan den/xam toi".

Mau kho (anh chup lung dinh tay/tuong, design nghieng): chep "cai-dat mau.json" vao thu muc
mockup, doi ten thanh "cai-dat.json", sua theo mau ben trong:
  vung  = x1,y1,x2,y2   chi lay design trong o nay (toa do pixel anh mockup)
  xoay  = -14           xoay thang (am = cung chieu kim dong ho)
  che   = ["x1,y1,x2,y2"]  to mau ao len cho dinh tay/tuong
  nen   = "#d1d0d0"     ep mau ao
  bo    = true          bo qua mau nay

Luu y: soat tung file truoc khi in - chi tiet nho do AI ve lai khi phong to.
